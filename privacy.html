Meridian Privacy Terms

Privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Meridian is a mail and calendar app for Mac and iPhone. It is designed so that your mail and calendar stay on your own devices. This policy explains what data Meridian accesses, where it goes, and how to remove it.

Data Meridian accesses

When you sign in with Google, Meridian requests these permissions:

  • Gmail: to list and display your messages, threads and labels (read), and, on the Mac, to send mail you write and to change labels on your conversations (archive, mark read or unread, star, move to Trash and back, snooze). Meridian never deletes mail permanently and cannot change your Gmail settings, filters or forwarding.
  • Google Calendar: to display your calendars and events, and to create, edit or delete events when you ask it to.
  • Google Contacts (read-only): to show names and photos for the people in your mail and calendar.
  • Basic profile (email, name): to identify which account is signed in.

Where your data goes

  • On your device. Meridian downloads your mail, calendar and contacts directly from Google to your Mac or iPhone and caches them there. Sign-in tokens are stored in your device's Keychain. Meridian does not operate a server that receives, stores or reads your mail, calendar or contacts.
  • Your iCloud account. If iCloud is enabled, Meridian syncs its own settings and state (for example snoozes, account colors, hidden calendars and preferences) between your devices through your private iCloud database. This never includes the contents of your email.
  • New-mail notifications. While new-mail notifications are on (you can turn them off in Settings), Meridian registers your device with a small relay service operated by the developer. The relay stores your Google account email address, your device's Apple push token, the platform and app version, and a random secret used to unregister. Gmail tells the relay only that your mailbox changed, never what changed. The relay then sends a silent push to your device, and your device fetches the new mail directly from Google. With the same setting on, Google Calendar also tells the relay when one of your calendars changes (never what changed), so your device can update your calendar right away; for each calendar it watches, the relay keeps a random identifier, a fingerprint of a random secret, and when the watch expires (after about a week), and never your events. Signing out of an account removes its registration and its calendar watches.
  • AI features. Smart features such as summaries and triage run on your device by default, using Apple Intelligence where it's available. If you choose a different AI engine for an account (Apple Private Cloud Compute, Claude from Anthropic with your own API key, or a custom AI server), the text of the messages it processes is sent to that provider, under that provider's terms. Nothing is sent to another provider unless you turn it on for that account.
  • Remote images. Images in emails are blocked by default. If you choose to load them, they are fetched from the sender's servers, or through an image proxy when one is configured, so the sender does not see your IP address.

Sending and changing mail

Nothing is sent or changed automatically. A message leaves only after you press Send and an undo window you choose (5 to 30 seconds) has passed. Each change Meridian makes in your Gmail or Google Calendar is recorded on your device (time, account, kind of change and conversation or event id, never the message content), so you can review it. The iPhone app does not send mail or change it.

What Meridian does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not use your data for advertising.
  • It does not use your Google data to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • It does not allow humans to read your data, except where you explicitly ask for help with a specific problem and grant access, where required for security purposes, or where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Meridian's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Sign out of an account in Meridian's settings to remove its sign-in tokens from the device and its notification registration from the relay.
  • Revoke Meridian's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data. Synced settings can be removed from iCloud in your device's iCloud storage settings.
  • To have a push relay registration removed, email the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
