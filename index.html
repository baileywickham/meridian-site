Meridian

A fast, native mail and calendar app for Mac and iPhone that works with your Google account.

Download Meridian 0.6.17 for Mac · macOS 27.0 or later · updates itself

What it does

  • Reads your Gmail, with keyboard-first triage: archive, star, snooze and reply without leaving the keyboard.
  • Shows your Google Calendar and lets you create, move and edit events. Before guests are emailed about a change, Meridian asks first.
  • Runs entirely on your devices. Meridian has no servers that store or read your mail or calendar.

Google account access

Meridian signs in with Google and asks for:

  • Gmail. Read your mail, send mail you write, and archive, star, mark read or move conversations to Trash. Meridian never deletes mail permanently or changes your Gmail settings.
  • Calendar. Read your calendars, and create, edit and delete events you ask it to.
  • Contacts, read-only. Names and photos for people in your mail and calendar.
  • Basic profile. Your email address and name, to identify the account.

See the privacy policy for exactly how this data is used.
